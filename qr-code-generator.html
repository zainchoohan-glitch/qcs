Types of QR Codes You Can Create

Different QR code types help you share different kinds of information. Choose the option that matches your needs.

Website URL QR Code

Create a QR code that opens a website when someone scans it with a smartphone. Use it on business cards, product packaging, flyers, posters, and promotional materials.

Text QR Code

Convert plain text into a QR code. This is useful for sharing short messages, instructions, notes, or other information without requiring someone to type it manually.

Email QR Code

Make it easier for people to contact you by creating an email QR code. When scanned, it can open the user's email application with your email information.

Phone QR Code

Create a QR code containing a phone number. People can scan it to access the number and start a call using their mobile device.

SMS QR Code

Generate an SMS QR code to make sending a text message easier. It can contain a phone number and a predefined message, depending on the supported QR format.

Where Can You Use QR Codes?

QR codes are useful in many everyday situations. Businesses can place them on menus, packaging, receipts, and promotional posters. Website owners can use them to share web pages, while individuals can add them to invitations, business cards, and personal documents. They also make it easier to share contact information and other details without manually typing long addresses or messages.

Tips for Creating Scannable QR Codes

  • Use a clear contrast between the QR code and its background.
  • Avoid making the QR code too small.
  • Keep enough empty space around the QR code.
  • Use a short URL when possible.
  • Test your QR code with a smartphone before printing.
  • Download a high-quality image for printed materials.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this QR code generator free?

Yes. You can use the QR code generator to create QR codes without paying for the basic generation feature.

Can I download my QR code?

Yes. The tool provides PNG and SVG download options for saving your generated QR code.

Do QR codes expire?

A static QR code does not have an automatic expiry date. However, if it contains a website URL, it will only work as expected while that destination remains available.

Can I create a QR code for my website?

Yes. Select the Website URL option, enter your website address, and generate your QR code.

Can I scan a QR code with my phone?

Yes. Most modern smartphones include a QR scanning feature in their camera application. Open your camera and point it at the QR code.

Which QR code image format should I download?

PNG is suitable for websites and everyday digital sharing. SVG is a vector format that can be resized for printing without losing image sharpness.
