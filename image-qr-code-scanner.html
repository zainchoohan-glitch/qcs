How to Scan a QR Code from an Image

If you have a QR code saved as a photo or screenshot, you can decode it using the image upload feature. Follow these steps:

  1. Open the Image QR Code Scanner on your device.
  2. Click the Upload Image button.
  3. Select a JPG, PNG, or SVG image containing a QR code.
  4. Wait for the scanner to process the uploaded image.
  5. View your scan result and copy the decoded information.

Scan QR Codes from Your Device Camera in the Browser

You can also scan QR codes directly with your device camera. Select the Camera Scanner option and click Start Camera. Allow camera permission when your browser requests it, then point your camera toward the QR code. The scanner will detect supported QR codes automatically.

Camera access may require permission from your browser. For security reasons, use a trusted connection and allow camera access only when you want to scan a QR code.

Where Can You Use an Image QR Scanner?

An image QR scanner is useful in many everyday situations. You can scan QR codes from saved screenshots, digital posters, product images, invitations, documents, and pictures received through messaging applications. It can also help you retrieve website addresses or information from QR codes without using a second phone to scan your screen.

Tips for Scanning QR Codes from Photos and Screenshots

  • Upload a clear image with a visible QR code.
  • Avoid blurry, distorted, or heavily cropped images.
  • Make sure the complete QR code is visible.
  • Use good lighting when scanning with your camera.
  • Keep your camera steady while scanning.
  • Check decoded URLs before opening them.

Is It Safe to Scan QR Codes from Images?

QR codes can contain website addresses and other information. Always check the decoded result before visiting a website. Avoid opening unfamiliar links or entering personal information on suspicious websites. Our scanner displays the decoded URL before you open it, helping you review the destination first.

Image QR Code Scanner FAQs

Can I scan a QR code from a screenshot?

Yes. Upload a screenshot containing a clear QR code using the image upload option. The scanner will attempt to decode the QR code from the image.

Can I scan a QR code without another phone?

Yes. If the QR code is saved as an image or screenshot on your device, you can upload that file directly to the scanner.

Which image formats are supported?

The image upload interface supports JPG, PNG, and SVG files containing QR codes.

Do I need to install an app?

No. You can use this online QR code scanner through a compatible web browser without installing a separate app.

Can I use this scanner on my smartphone?

Yes. You can open the scanner in a compatible mobile browser on an iPhone or Android device. Camera scanning requires browser camera permission.

Is it safe to scan QR codes?

Scanning a QR code does not automatically make its destination safe. Review the decoded information and verify website addresses before opening links.
