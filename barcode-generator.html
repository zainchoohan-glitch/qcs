02 Which Barcode Format Should You Choose?

▥

Retail Products

EAN-13 · EAN-8 · UPC-A · UPC-E

These formats are commonly used at retail store checkouts. UPC-A uses 12 digits and is common in North America, while EAN-13 uses 13 digits and is widely used internationally. EAN-8 and UPC-E are shorter formats designed for smaller packaging.

Retail product identifiers are assigned through GS1. A barcode generator creates the barcode image but does not provide ownership of an official product number.

▤

Shipping & Inventory

Code 128 · Code 39 · ITF

Code 128 supports a wide range of characters in a compact barcode, making it useful for shipping labels and asset tracking. Code 39 is a common alphanumeric format used in industrial applications. ITF encodes digits and is often printed on cartons.

▦

Small Items, IDs & Tickets

Data Matrix · PDF417 · Aztec · QR Code

These two-dimensional barcode formats can store more information in a compact area. Data Matrix is useful for small components, PDF417 is used on identification documents and passes, Aztec is common on transport tickets, and QR Codes can be scanned by smartphone cameras.

03 Tips for Barcodes That Scan Reliably

01

Use dark bars on a plain, light background and leave enough empty space around the code.

02

Scale the image proportionally. Never stretch or squeeze the barcode.

03

Print at a suitable size and test a sample before printing in bulk.

04

Choose a barcode format supported by your scanner or receiving system.

04 Barcode Generator FAQ

Is this barcode generator free?

Yes. There is no account to create and no limit on how many barcodes you can make, subject to normal browser and device limitations.

Are my barcode values stored?

No. Barcode values are processed in your browser and are not uploaded to a server by the generator.

What file format can I download?

You can download your generated barcode as a PNG image.

Can I use these barcodes in a store?

Retail barcodes such as EAN and UPC require valid product identifiers issued through GS1 for products sold in stores. For internal inventory, you can use Code 128 or QR Codes with your own numbering system.
